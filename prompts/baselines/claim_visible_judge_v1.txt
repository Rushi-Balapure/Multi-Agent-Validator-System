Use only the supplied evidence. If the evidence is empty or does not decide the claim, choose NEI.

- REFUTE when the evidence contradicts the claim
- NEI when the evidence is missing or not enough

Also give a confidence between 0 and 1 for the chosen label.

Return JSON only:
{"label": "SUPPORT|REFUTE|NEI", "rationale": "<one or two sentences>", "confidence": 0.0}
